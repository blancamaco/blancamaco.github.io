FACEIT Voice & Chat

Improving real-time communication for competitive gaming through clarity, usability, and trust.

Role: Product Designer

Team: Product Managers, Engineers, Audio Engineers

Focus: Voice & Chat Experience

Overview

During my time on the Voice and Chat teams at FACEIT, I led multiple initiatives to enhance communication features for competitive gaming. This case study highlights key features that improved usability, engagement, and clarity in voice and chat experiences, helping players communicate seamlessly in real-time.

Project Background

The Challenge

FACEIT's competitive gaming platform required robust voice and chat solutions to support thousands of players communicating in real-time. The existing solution had scalability issues and users often didn’t notice or trust the communication tools.

My Role

I was responsible for designing and implementing features that improved the communication experience. This included user research, prototyping, usability testing, and collaboration with engineers and PMs.

Improved Voice Controls & Members Panel

Problem

Players often didn't know who was speaking in voice chat, causing missed conversations and reduced engagement.

Decisions

The solution had to be visible but not intrusive and live where players already looked for context: the members panel.

Solution

  • Real-time indicators showing active speakers
  • Inline audio status icons (muted, deafened, active)
  • Subtle pulse animation on the speaker avatar

Impact

Players immediately understood who was speaking, improving voice engagement.

Connection Indicator

Problem

Players couldn't tell if poor voice quality was caused by their connection, teammates, or the platform.

Decisions

Keep it simple, glanceable, and expandable only when deeper info was needed.

Solution

  • Real-time connection status indicator
  • Expandable panel for advanced diagnostics
  • Clear guidance on improving quality

Impact

Significant reduction in voice-related support tickets.

Chat Improvements

Problem

The chat system lacked hierarchy, clarity, and visual consistency.

Decisions

Modernize without rebuilding: improve hierarchy and readability using visual design.

Solution

  • Cleaner message layout
  • Reusable UI components
  • Improved spacing and structure

Impact

Easier-to-read chat that established a foundation for future features.

Voice Settings

Problem

The existing voice settings were too limited for competitive players.

Decisions

Expand customization while keeping onboarding simple.

Solution

  • Device configuration & testing
  • Noise suppression, latency, push-to-talk
  • Quick setup for first-time users

Key Learnings

Just because we can technically build a feature doesn’t mean we should. Most users wanted fast setup and clarity, not professional audio complexity.

This reinforced the importance of balancing ambition with real user needs.
