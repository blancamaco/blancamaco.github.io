A little bit about myself

Since I was a child, I have been painting, drawing, filming and creating all kinds of things. When my father brought a computer to home, I discovered endless ways it could help me to create those things and many more.

That curiosity led me to study Computer Engineering, where I learned how things work behind the screen. In parallel, I started designing visually interfaces for side projects and startups, which helped me discover my true passion: the intersection between design and technology.

Over the years, I’ve built digital products that blend thoughtful design with clean, functional implementation. Today, I continue growing as a Product Designer at ESL FACEIT Group, and UX Engineer on my free time, crafting interactive experiences and bridging the gap between design and development to bring ideas to life in the most seamless way possible.

You can find my detailed experience down below, in my CV or my LinkedIn.
