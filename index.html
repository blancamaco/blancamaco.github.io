Since I was a child, I have been painting, drawing, filming and creating all kinds of things. When my father brought a computer to home, I discovered endless ways it could help me to create those things and many more.

After high-school, I decided to study Computer Engineering so I could understand technology better, while in my free time I exploited my creativity working as a visual designer on side projects and startups. It was then when I discovered the UX/UI field, a mixture of my two major interests, design and technology, by studing how people interact with technological products. That encouraged me to study a Design Master and take some online UX/UI courses after my Bachelor to gain more knowledge about the field.

Nowadays I keep learning everyday and creating better digital products as a Product Designer at ESL FACEIT Group.

You can find my detailed experience down below, in my CV or my LinkedIn.
